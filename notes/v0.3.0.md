## YouTube's own player

When YouTube refuses to hand a song to the app ("confirm you're not a bot"), the song now plays on YouTube's own page (music.youtube.com, or m.youtube.com if you pick it), kept hidden behind the app. The app only presses play, pause and seek on that page; it does not get around YouTube's check.

- **Settings → YouTube player → Open** shows the page. Sign in there or answer YouTube once; songs then play through it. Signing in to YouTube Music in the app signs the page in too.
- **When to use it:** only when YouTube refuses the app (default), always, or never.
- Songs played this way skip the equalizer and can't be downloaded. YouTube may show ads, and without YouTube Premium the page may pause when the screen locks.
- The full-screen player shows "через YouTube" (or "реклама") under the progress bar while this is happening.
