Unsigned build for sideloading with Sideloadly, AltStore or SideStore. See УСТАНОВКА.md. Installs over 0.2.0; likes and playlists stay.

## Changes

- **New full-screen player.** It no longer spills past the screen edges. Close it with the arrow at the top; the more menu is in the top corner. Swipe the cover for the next or previous song. The progress bar is thicker and can be dragged anywhere. Lyrics and the queue show a small cover above them. Lyrics, sleep timer, equalizer and queue sit in one bar at the bottom.
- **Search:** the Songs / Artists / Albums / Playlists switch no longer slides under the search field.
- **Messages:** when YouTube refuses a song, the message names the song and also shows while the full-screen player is open.

YouTube now often asks anonymous players from home IPs to confirm they are not a bot. The app does not get around that check. Songs on the phone always play; switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data, or turning off a VPN, usually helps.
