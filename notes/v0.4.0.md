Unsigned build for sideloading with Sideloadly, AltStore or SideStore. See [the installation guide](https://github.com/Hkotuk5/hkotuner-ios/blob/v0.4.0/УСТАНОВКА.md). Installs over 0.3.0 with the same signing identity and bundle identifier.

## Accounts and sync

- Register, sign in or continue as a guest from the animated welcome screen. Signing out opens that screen again.
- A persistent connection tells the app when your library or profile changes. Edits sync automatically between signed-in devices while they are connected.
- Transfer playback between iPhone and a connected desktop.

## Playback and appearance

- Search SoundCloud and open supported direct HTTPS audio links.
- Seekable audio starts with buffering and the next track is prepared in the background.
- Keep supported tracks offline from the track menu, including eligible favorites in bulk.
- Crop your avatar, use a GIF avatar and customize the theme or wallpaper. Lyrics highlight in time with playback.

## YouTube downloads

YouTube can require verification or temporarily limit downloads. The app explains these responses and skips further YouTube network downloads in the current batch while continuing with other sources. Open the YouTube player in settings for online playback when verification is required. Tracks available only through that page cannot be saved in hkotuner.
