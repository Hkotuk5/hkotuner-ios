Unsigned build for sideloading with Sideloadly, AltStore or SideStore. See УСТАНОВКА.md. Installs over 0.1.0; likes and playlists stay.

## What's new

- **YouTube Music sign-in** on Google's own page, in a private web view (the app never sees the password). Brings over the account's likes and playlists and adds personal shelves on Home. Audio is fetched the same way as without signing in.
- **Bot check:** if YouTube asks to confirm you're not a bot, the app suggests turning off the VPN or switching network and moves on to songs already on the phone. Nothing is bypassed.
- **Browse:** artist and album pages, follow artists, "New from your artists", search for artists, albums and playlists, playlists from YouTube and Spotify links.
- **Sound:** synced lyrics (LRCLIB), 10-band equalizer with the desktop presets, loudness levelling, sleep timer, repeat one or the whole queue, drag to reorder the queue, playback resumes where it stopped after a restart.
- **Library:** share links and `.hkplaylist` files in the desktop's format, playlist covers, "Often played" and "Not heard in a while", listening stats, weekly backup to Files, update check.
- **Design:** every screen redone for the phone, light theme and accent colours.
