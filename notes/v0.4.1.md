Unsigned iPhone build for AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly. See [the installation guide](https://github.com/Hkotuk5/hkotuner-ios/blob/v0.4.1/%D0%A3%D0%A1%D0%A2%D0%90%D0%9D%D0%9E%D0%92%D0%9A%D0%90.md).

- Follow lyrics word by word, with exact timings when available and estimates for line-timed lyrics.
